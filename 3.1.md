# Next: Slide 2 — Why did Google need B4?

This is where your seminar **actually begins**.

The key idea is surprisingly simple:

```text
Traditional WAN

Lots of expensive capacity
          ↓
Keep utilization low
          ↓
Survive failures
          ↓
Very reliable
          ↓
But expensive + wasted capacity
```

The paper says WAN links were typically provisioned at only **30–40% average utilization**, resulting in roughly **2–3× bandwidth over-provisioning**. 

And **this is the problem B4 tries to attack.**
